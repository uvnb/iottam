CAREPOSTURE PI 4 GUI - TEAM DMT
================================

MUC DICH
- GUI chi hien thi ket qua 5 tu the, khong doc MPU va khong chay AI.
- Da chua san ham nhan lenh de sau nay gan BLE/AI.
- Toi uu cho man hinh ngang 1024x600 cua Raspberry Pi 4.

5 LENH HO TRO
bad_posture
bending
lifting_wrong_back
normal_idle
shoulder_asymmetry

CAI DAT TREN RASPBERRY PI OS
1. Mo Terminal tai thu muc chuong trinh.
2. Cai Tkinter va Pillow:
   sudo apt update
   sudo apt install -y python3-tk python3-pil python3-pil.imagetk

3. Chay GUI:
   python3 CarePosture_Pi4_GUI.py

Chay thu truc tiep mot tu the:
   python3 CarePosture_Pi4_GUI.py bad_posture

PHIM DEMO
1 = bad_posture
2 = bending
3 = lifting_wrong_back
4 = normal_idle
5 = shoulder_asymmetry
F11 = bat/tat toan man hinh
Esc = thoat toan man hinh; bam lan nua de dong GUI

DIEM GAN BLE/AI SAU NAY
Trong callback nhan duoc lenh, chi can goi:
   app.receive_posture_command("bad_posture")

Ham chap nhan ca bytes, vi du:
   app.receive_posture_command(b"shoulder_asymmetry")

Khong can thay doi giao dien hay logic hieu ung.
